The stack works by itself within what you allow, and asks you only by exception. Every kind of action has a tier:
it **does it**, **does it and tells you**, **asks you first**, or **never** does it. Everything that acts on your
things starts at "asks you first".

## A question, answered in place

Each question is one card in **Needs you** (on Home, and in full under **All**). The question comes first; the
small **i** shows why it matters, its facts and what happens without an answer.

- **Tap a chip** to answer. The card says so at once, and **Undo** stays for a few seconds before the stack acts.
- **Answer in your own words** in the box: "Single, as always", "about 23 km, most days", "ask me again in January".
  The stack shows what it understood, with Undo, or asks one short question back when it is not sure.
- **Later** puts a question off (in an hour, tonight, tomorrow morning, next week); **Dismiss** lets it go.
- **Always for this kind**, then a chip: your answer, and a standing rule for every question of that kind.
- Questions of one kind come as one card, with one answer for all and each item's own chips.
- When the stack is restarting, your answer is **kept** and sent once when it is back; nothing is answered twice.

A **gate** (deploying a change, buying, deleting, granting access, and what a work deployment marks so) counts only a
tap on its chip by a person who holds its role. No rule, default or reading of your words answers it; your words are
kept as a comment.

## What the stack may do

**What the stack may do** (from Needs you) lists each kind of question with its tier, your standing rules (who made
each and when, with Remove), what the stack did for you on its own with its Undo, and the **stop switch**.

**Stop everything** (here and on Home) holds every agent run, workflow step, plan step and crew job the stack would
start, and pauses the swarm; what runs now finishes. The banner says since when and what it held; **Resume** lets it
all go on.
