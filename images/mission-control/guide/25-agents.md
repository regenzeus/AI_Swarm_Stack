An **agent** is a building block: a role with its instructions, model, tools, skills, knowledge and budget. It only
works as a step of a workflow's run, and one agent can be a step of many workflows.

## Agents

The **Agents** page lists them by their job. Its other parts: **Health and results** (each scheduled job's health and
the measured results per agent and kind), **Who works with whom** (which agents worked on which runs, and the agents
together) and **What agents know** (the memory: wikis, long-term facts, how-to, journals).

```try
label: See the agents
open: "#/agents"
note: Tap an agent for its page and the workflows it is used in.
```

A change to an agent (its model, its tools, its instructions) is tested on its past runs first and waits for your tap;
nothing changes by itself.

## Ideas

Under **Code**, **Ideas** are the agents' proposals to make the stack and themselves better, each with its evidence; nothing starts
without your tap. The **measures** of your records (a pack's tracker) and the **benchmarks** (practice runs with an
answer key no agent reads) are one tap from Workflows. Scorers, rubrics that grade a sample of runs, come later.

```try
label: See what could be better
open: "#/ideas"
note: The open proposals first, then what was decided lately.
```
