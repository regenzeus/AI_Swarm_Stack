Do these in order. Each one takes a minute or two and leaves nothing behind that you'd need to clean up.
Every **Try it** card opens the right place for you. Chat examples are typed in for you but not sent, so you can
read them and change them first.

## 1. Ask your assistant what's going on

Hermes is your assistant. It knows your missions, reports, wikis and notes, and can act on them.

```try
label: Ask Hermes for a status update
chat: What are my missions doing right now, and did anything interesting happen today?
note: Hermes looks up your missions with its tools and answers in a few seconds. You'll see the steps it takes (for example missions_overview) above the answer.
```

## 2. Read your latest briefing

Briefings are written by the Topic Desk, usually every morning. Open a mission and read its latest report.

```try
label: Open your missions
open: "#/tasks"
note: Tap a mission, then the Results tab. Each report links its sources.
```

## 3. Explore a knowledge graph

Every mission builds its own wiki: companies, people, concepts and events, linked together like in Obsidian.

```try
label: Open the wiki graph
open: "#/wiki"
note: Switch to Graph and tap a dot to open that page. Links and backlinks are at the bottom of each page.
```

## 4. Give it a small job

Ask for something that takes real research. Hermes turns it into a one-off mission, does the research, and
sends you the result.

```try
label: Ask for a short research note
chat: Research what happened to copper spot prices this week and send me a short summary in the app.
note: Hermes creates a one-off mission. You'll see it under Missions while it runs, then get the result as a notification.
```

## 5. Put it on your phone

{{APP_NAME}} installs like an app and sends push notifications for finished runs, events and errors.

```try
label: Set up this device
open: "#/system"
note: Scroll to "This device". On iPhone, first use Share → Add to Home Screen, open the app from there, then switch on push.
```

### Share anything to your stack

Once the app is installed on Android, **{{APP_NAME}} appears in every app's Share menu**. Share a link, a
product page, a screenshot, a PDF or some text, and the app opens on it with one-tap choices: summarise it, ask what
to do with it, explain it simply, ask Hermes anything about it, save it to your knowledge, or turn it into a task.
Nothing happens until you tap; shared items are kept for two weeks. Long-press the app icon for shortcuts to Chat,
New task, Stack and Code.

> [!note] Installed the app before this feature? Remove it from the home screen and install it again once, so
> Android picks up the Share entry.

> [!tip] That's the core loop: **ask, let it work, get notified, read.** The rest of the guide goes deeper into
> each part. Start with **Chat with Hermes** or **Tasks**.
